    Why [Ruins/Lair/Throne] appear twice:
      Loop 3 wraps around — Ruins connects EAST to Tower (mountains, upper-left)
      and SOUTH to Lair (dark zone, lower-right). Both positions are shown.

    Why Camp is not directly below Gate:
      Gate W (left) -> Camp.  Gate S (down) -> Forest.
      Camp is placed LEFT of Gate, Forest is placed BELOW Gate.
      Camp also connects N->Clearing and S->Arena (below it).
      Clearing W->Deep_F closes Loop 2.

    Underground passages (Loop 3):
      Tunnel (mountains) --E/W-- Catacombs (dark zone)
      Tower  (mountains) --W/E-- Ruins     (dark zone)
      Circuit: Cavern->Peak->Tower···>Ruins->Catacombs···>Tunnel->Cavern
